On the dunes at noon, 38 percent of caustic pixels hit the top of the stock filmic curve and turn flat white. The caustic pattern, which is the whole point of the effect, disappears where it is brightest. The curve was the wrong choice for saltglass from the start; its shoulder was tuned for skies, not for small, very bright sources.
