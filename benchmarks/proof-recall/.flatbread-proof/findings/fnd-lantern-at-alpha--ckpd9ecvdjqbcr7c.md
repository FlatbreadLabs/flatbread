Alpha landed on 2026-03-02. The visibility buffer now resolves materials in compute, and foliage is back in the buffer thanks to meshlet alpha masks; saltglass still renders forward.

Virtual shadow maps replaced cascaded shadow maps on 2026-02-16 after the 3.1 ms cost Finding turned out to be a capture error. SDF GI remains the accepted GI Decision, but the recapture on a Meridian devkit measured 4.7 ms, not 2.2 ms, and nobody has re-weighed the GI Decision. Refraction is screen-space with a ray-marched fallback. Caustics flicker under Tern; the blocker is open with two proposed fixes. Tonemapping moved to the Brine curve. Tern ships three presets. The Frame budget gives GPU passes 15.2 ms.
