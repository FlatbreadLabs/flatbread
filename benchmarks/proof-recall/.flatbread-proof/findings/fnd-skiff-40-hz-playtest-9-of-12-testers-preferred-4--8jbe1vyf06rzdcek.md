Dara Whitlock ran 12 testers through the dune route on Skiff at 30 Hz and at 40 Hz, in random order. 9 preferred 40 Hz, 2 saw no difference, 1 preferred 30 Hz for battery life. Battery drain rose from 11 to 13 percent an hour.
