The sun drives most of the light on the coast, and saltglass makes shadow errors easy to see because refraction bends them. Candidates: cascaded shadow maps, virtual shadow maps, or ray-traced sun shadows. Shadows must reach 2 km and fit the Frame budget on Meridian.
