Lantern is Kestrel's renderer. This Effort covers its overhaul for Saltglass Reach: the move to a visibility buffer, sun and local shadows, global illumination, saltglass refraction and caustics, the Tern upscaler, HDR and tonemapping, GPU-driven culling, and the Meridian frame budgets including the 120 Hz performance mode. Ines Okafor leads it.
