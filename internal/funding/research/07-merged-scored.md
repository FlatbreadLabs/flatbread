Opportunity uses one global 100-point scale: cash 40, credits 30, relevant exposure 20, and research latitude 10. UNKNOWN per-applicant cash or credit value scores 0. Known ranges use the highest published endpoint available to Flatbread's current solo applicant: up to $5k=8; >$5k–$25k=16; >$25k–$100k=26 cash/24 credits; >$100k–$500k=34 cash/30 credits; cash >$500k=40. Confidence is the chance of a usable result from action available now; it is zero when no application can currently be submitted or a hard current gate is unmet. EV is `round(Opportunity × Confidence / 100)`.
