## Sequencing risks

- **Separate every work package before applying.** Sovereign Tech bars other public financing for the same proposed activities. Prototype Fund says no double funding. NSF requires disclosure of identical or substantially similar proposals. EIC cannot pay the same costs twice. NLnet permits combined finance but not retroactive work. Sources: https://www.sovereign.tech/programs/fund, https://www.prototypefund.de/en/faq, https://www.nsf.gov/policies/pappg/24-1/ch-2-proposal-preparation, https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator_en and https://nlnet.nl/restack/faq/
- **Use distinct scopes.** A safe split is: maintainer time and packaging for an OSS grant; API runs and benchmark data for a credit program; research design and publication for a research grant. Do not send the same “48-run bounded recall evaluation” budget to two funders.
- **Do not take venture money by accident.** A SAFE does not automatically bar NSF, EIC or NLnet, but it changes Flatbread from an unfunded public-good project into a venture company, can move it out of unfunded credit tiers, and creates investor claims on the roadmap. Sources: https://www.ycombinator.com/deal, https://cloud.google.com/startup/faq and https://nlnet.nl/faq/
- **Finish the harness before starting expiring credits.** Instrument provider calls, input and output tokens, cache use, latency and cash cost first. Then run the 12-run baseline and only then accept 90-day Arcee or six-month OpenRouter credits. Sources: https://www.arcee.ai/trinity-builders-program and https://openrouter.ai/terms/startup-program
- **Choose a foundation home only after deciding asset ownership.** Moving to OpenJS, CNCF or LF AI can transfer marks or assets and impose governance. That decision should precede, not follow, applications that describe Tony as the sole owner and decision-maker. Sources: https://github.com/openjs-foundation/cross-project-council/blob/main/PROJECT_PROGRESSION.md and https://contribute.cncf.io/projects/lifecycle/
- **Do not overlap full-time fellowships.** MATS, Anthropic Fellows and Astera each expect substantial or full-time participation.
