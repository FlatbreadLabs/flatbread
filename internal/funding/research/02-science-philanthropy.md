## Coverage note

This run opened more than forty funder or programme pages and kept seventeen ranked entries. Six of those seventeen are closed or between calls and are listed because their cash or credit size is large enough that a later window matters. Coverage is strongest on US and UK AI-safety philanthropy that still takes individuals (TAIF, Manifund, Lightcone Commons, Coefficient career and capacity tracks, SFF rolling form, Foresight, Astera). Coverage is thin on continental European research-software fellowships, on invitation-only science foundations whose portals need a login, and on Sloan’s two main software subprogramme pages, which did not load. Academic-only lines (Simons software faculty, Wellcome career awards, Schmidt science fellowships) were opened far enough to screen, not to hunt hidden exceptions. Industry-adjacent vehicles (OpenAI Foundation, Frontier Model Forum remainder funds) have no public apply path on the pages opened. The honest apply list for a solo maintainer with no known legal entity, no fiscal host, and no university appointment is short: Lightcone Commons today and then quarterly, TAIF rolling, Manifund public proposal, SFF rolling speculation form, Coefficient career-development if Tony wants a personal transition grant, and watch-and-wait for Astera, Foresight Nodes, and a non-life-sciences OS4Science call after a fiscal host exists.
